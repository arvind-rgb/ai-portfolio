Fund Raiser

AI investor-outreach CRM

Built to run a seed raise as a solo founder: it finds angels who might actually back the business, writes a personal first message to each one in my voice, and tracks every touch until the investor replies or says no.

  • LLM lead scoring with an angels-only gate and layered de-duplication
  • Enrichment routing by person vs. firm
  • Personalised email and LinkedIn drafts that only use verified facts
  • Templates, outreach tracking and a funnel report
