ShipHop.AI

Make couriers compete for your parcel

A multi-tenant last-mile logistics portal running in production: one search returns fares from six courier networks cheapest-first, books the winner in one click, tracks the rider live and reports on spend.

  • Parallel rate sweep across 6 couriers, cheapest first
  • One-click booking with live rider, vehicle, OTP and status tracking
  • Encrypted credentials (AES-256-GCM), JWT auth, multi-tenant admin
  • Reports with one-click PDF export; reconnect UX for expired sessions
