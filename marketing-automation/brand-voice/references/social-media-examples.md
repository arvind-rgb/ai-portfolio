## Reels / Short-form Video Caption Template

**Structure:**
- Hook (1 sentence — the human, not the food)
- Brief story (2-3 sentences about the chef or dish's origin)
- Sensory pull (what it smells/tastes/feels like)
- Soft CTA (discovery-led, not transactional)

**Example:**

She quit her corporate job to cook Bihari food for strangers.

Best decision anyone ever made.

Anita's Sattu Paratha — hand-ground roasted gram, stuffed with green chili and mustard oil — is the kind of breakfast your body remembers even when your mind forgets.

Find Anita on Bhookle. 🫙
