## Footer / Mission Statement

**Short version (below CTA):**
*Bhookle was born from a search for a mother's cooking — and the realization that millions carry the same hunger. We exist to bridge memory and a meal.*

**Long version (About page):**

Arvind built Bhookle after losing his mother.

He found himself searching for her dishes — the specific texture of her dal, the smell of her spice box, the way her food carried love in every layer. He realized this search is universal: millions of people across India carry that same craving for food made by someone who cares, and can't find it anywhere.

Restaurants gave us 15 standard dishes and called it variety. Bhookle gives you 19,500+ dishes and the human behind every one of them.

We are the Social Marketplace for home-cooked food.
We are India's Trusted Second Kitchen.
We are Amma in a Dabba.
