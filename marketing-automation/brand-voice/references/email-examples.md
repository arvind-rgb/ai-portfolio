# Email Examples — Gold Standard

These examples set the quality bar for Bhookle email campaigns.
The tone is a trusted friend sharing a discovery — never a newsletter, never a promotion blast.

## Subject Line Swipe File (for future campaigns)

**Curiosity-led:**
- `India has 1000s of dishes no restaurant will ever serve you`
- `What happened to the food you ate at your grandmother's house?`
- `Why does home food taste different? (It's not just nostalgia)`

**Story-led:**
- `Rekha makes Sindhi Kadhi every Tuesday. 43 people are waiting.`
- `She cooks for 15 strangers every day. And she loves every one of them.`

**Contrast / antidote angle:**
- `The 16th time you ordered Dal Makhani this month…`
- `Not a restaurant. A kitchen that loves you back.`

**FOMO / scarcity (human-led, not urgency-led):**
- `Priya's Malabar Biryani is only available Fridays. Today is Thursday.`
