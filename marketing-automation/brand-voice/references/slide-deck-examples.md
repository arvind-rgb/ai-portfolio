# Slide Deck Examples — Gold Standard

These examples set the quality bar for Bhookle presentation copy —
investor decks, brand presentations, partner pitches, and internal keynotes.

Slide copy follows the brand's visual language: big contrast statements, punchy tension,
declarative truth. Every headline should be able to stand alone as a provocation.

---

## Slide 1 — Problem Statement (Opening Tension)

**Headline:**
> "Food Ordering Today Is Mostly About Ordering.
> And Less About the Food."

**Supporting copy:**
There are 21 meal opportunities a week. Food apps give you the same 20 options for all of them.
The same Dal Makhani. The same Chicken Tikka. The same reheated, processed, factory-kitchen plate.

Consumers are tired. They just don't have an alternative.
*Until now.*

---

## Slide 2 — The Real Problem (Depth)

**Headline:**
> "India Has 1000s of Dishes. Restaurants Serve 15 of Them."

**Supporting copy:**
India is the most culinarily diverse country on earth.
Sindhi Kadhi. Bihari Litti. Malabar Thalassery Biryani. Tamil Kumbakonam Kadappa.

Commercialization has erased them from menus.
They survive only in home kitchens — cooked by women who learned from their mothers,
who learned from theirs.

Bhookle is the first platform to give these dishes — and the people who make them — a table.

## Headline Formula for New Slides

Bhookle slide headlines work on tension and contrast. Use these patterns:

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| **Contrast** | "Most platforms focus on speed. We focus on the cook." |
| **Statistic as provocation** | "67% of our dishes exist nowhere else in the world of food apps." |
| **Quote as headline** | "She said her first order was the first time her family saw her as more than a housewife." |
| **The Reframe** | "This isn't food delivery. This is memory delivery." |
| **Simple declarative truth** | "Food cooked with love tastes different. We have 6,700 customers who can prove it." |
