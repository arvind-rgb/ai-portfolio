# Ad Copy Examples — Gold Standard

These examples set the quality bar for Bhookle paid media copy.
Ads lead with emotion or a provocation — never price or generic benefit claims.
Always anchor identity ("Real food. Real moms.") somewhere in the creative.

## Ad Copy Rules (Quick Reference)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Never open with price or discount | Cheapens the brand and attracts wrong audience |
| Always name a chef or cuisine when possible | Specificity creates trust and curiosity |
| Lead with the problem or emotional hook | Creates the need before the solution |
| End with an identity anchor | "Real food. Real moms." / "Amma in a Dabba" grounds the brand |
| CTA should invite discovery, not command purchase | "Discover her kitchen" > "Order now" |
