They are [ACTION: plating / stirring / slicing] [DISH NAME] with confident, practiced hands.
Natural window light from the left. Warm ochre and cream tones. Real kitchen textures — steel
vessels, clay pots, handwritten masala labels. Camera slowly pulls back to reveal the full scene.
Text overlay at 0s: "[POST HOOK]" in bold white with subtle drop shadow.
Cut at 5s to extreme close-up of the dish. Cut at 10s back to chef smiling directly at camera.
End card at 25s: Bhookle logo bottom-right, "Order now" CTA.
Background audio: [TRENDING SOUND NAME from Trend Scout]. Duration: 30s. Aspect ratio: 9:16.
```

```
Image generation prompt:

Clay bowl or steel thali. Ingredients scattered artfully around — [LIST 3 INGREDIENTS].
Natural daylight from top-left. Warm colour palette: ochre, terracotta, cream, dark green.

No stock-photo feel. Gritty, real, editorial food photography style.
